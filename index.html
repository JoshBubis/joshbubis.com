About

Engineering practice

Automated tests around the paths that matter, code review before merge, and deploys that go out on a push rather than a ritual. Secrets stay in managed stores, never in a repository.

I keep production observable — health checks, structured logs, and alerting that pages a human only when something genuinely needs one.
